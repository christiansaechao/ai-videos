ASSEMBLY

CapCut checklist — same for all three

  1. Import clips in order, trim each to its noted length — cut every dead frame at the start of a clip.
  2. Screen recordings: crop to the phone's frame, speed 1.2–1.5×, add a subtle slow zoom-in.
  3. Captions: bold, high-contrast, center-bottom, 2–4 words per line, timed to the beat or VO.
  4. Audio: trending sound at ~20% under VO (Script 3) or ~70% with no VO (Scripts 1–2) — pull the sound from TikTok's Creative Center the day you post, not in advance.
  5. Export 1080×1920, post natively per platform, and log 3-second view-through rate per video against the retention benchmarks (70% = distribution threshold).
