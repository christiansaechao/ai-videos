## 1. Headline findings

The through-line across every 2026 study: platforms now punish anything that reads as an
ad and reward content that earns the view first and sells as a side effect.

**The authenticity premium, measured:**
| Measurement | Organic-feeling vs. polished |
|---|---|
| UGC-style ads vs. traditional ad formats (TikTok) | **3× overall performance** |
| Completion rate, UGC-style vs. polished brand content | **2.4×** |
| Creator-led ads vs. brand-produced — CTR | **+70%** |
| Unscripted vs. polished influencer content — engagement | **+62%** |
| Spark (boosted-organic) ads vs. studio in-feed — CVR | **2.6% vs 1.8% (+44%)** |
| Unscripted vs. polished — conversion | **+38%** |
| Lo-fi TikTok ads — watch-through | **+32%** |
| Raw Reels vs. studio-quality | **+20%** |

**Context numbers:** viewer decides in ~1.7s; #deinfluencing >12B views (audience actively
hostile to pitches); creator trust at 67% (Nielsen) — 18–34s now rank creator content
their single most-trusted source, above search and peer reviews; a trend peaks in ~72h and
is dead in ~5 days (speed > polish).

## 2. Retention math (decides distribution)

- Watch time/completion ≈ **40–50% of TikTok's ranking decision**; ~70% retention at 3s
  triggers wider distribution.
- 3s retention by hook strength: weak ~30–40%, moderate ~60–70%, strong ~80–90%.
  Videos ≥85% at 3s earn **~2.8× total views** vs. <60%.
- Completion by length: <15s ≈ 60–70%, 15–30s ≈ 50–60% (**our target zone**),
  30–60s ≈ 40–50%, 60s+ ≈ 30–40%.
- Loop-friendly endings get **65% more rewatches**; rewatch >15–20% is elite.

## 3. AI-content rule for our pipeline

36% of consumers say catching a brand secretly using AI video lowers their perception —
but the penalty largely disappears with upfront use (transparent AI measures up to a 73%
trust lift vs. stealth AI). Fake AI "customers" also carry FTC fake-review exposure (up
to $51,744/violation).

**Production rule:** Kling generates *scenes* (driveways, worksites, b-roll, atmosphere);
real screen recordings prove the *product*; anything persuading with a "person" is either
the founder's real face or obviously stylized. Never a fake human posing as a customer.
The winning 2026 formats mostly don't need a talking human — which is exactly what an
image→video pipeline is good at.

## 4. The stealth-ad format catalog (ranked)

| # | Format | Evidence | Kling fit | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | **Day-in-the-life / operator vlog** — app appears for 3s as set dressing | #dayinmylife+#dailyvlog ≈35B views; WildHats 31× category interactions on process content | Easy (scene shots + 1 screen moment) | Reach |
| F2 | **Storytime pivot** — "client swore he paid me" → receipt trail resolves the plot | Storytime = top authenticity format for business accounts; embodies the brand name | Medium (multi-shot + VO) | Story |
| F3 | **Oddly-satisfying process b-roll + text-overlay narrative** — product only in final overlay | Before/after = most shared/saved service-business format; text-on-aesthetic is top-10 2026 | Easiest, cheapest at volume | Reach |
| F4 | **Reply-to-comment Q&A** — pinned question answered with screen recording | Comment-reply extends video lifespan, outsized engagement | Hybrid (screen + you) | Convert |
| F5 | **Faceless "3 apps" listicle — us at #3** — two honest third-party recs buy credibility | AI-narrated listicle-over-b-roll is a standing 2026 format; matches existing shorts pipeline | Easy | Story |
| F6 | **Greenscreen cost breakdown** — "$2,400/yr for a one-person business?" over Jobber pricing page | Standing hot-take format; video port of the AEO comparison-page wedge | Needs founder's face | Convert |
| F7 | **POV micro-skit** — "POV: you say yes to 'do you take card?'" | POV engineered for comments; specificity is everything | Medium | Reach |
| F8 | **Street interview** — skip for now: lives on real spontaneity; AI version = fake-human penalty | — | Skip | — |

## 5. Funnel assembly

| Layer | Formats | Product visibility |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | F3, F7, F1 | ~0–10% |
| Story | F2, F5 | ~20–30% |
| Convert | F4, F6 | ~60–80% |

Classic mistake = publishing only the convert layer — precisely the content 2026 audiences
scroll past. Entertain first, educate second, sell last; the ratio should be steep.
