## Rocco's Signature Format (memorize this — it's the brand)

Four shots. Same shape every video, swap the niche. ~20 seconds total.

| Shot | Job | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — HOOK** | Dismiss the normal path + drop an absurd claim | Must stop the scroll in 3 seconds |
| **2 — ESCALATE** | An impossible/inflated number; the number often slips into honesty | Background exposes the lie |
| **3 — THE SECRET** | His scammy "mechanism" — the tell that he's a fraud | Lower the voice like a secret |
| **4 — BUTTON** | Three-verb CTA + signature **"…You're welcome."** | Same closer every time |

### Recurring signature elements (do NOT change these — repetition = recognition)
- **Closer:** always ends on smug **"…You're welcome."**
- **Rule-of-three CTA:** three barked verbs before the closer (*"Point. Spray. Retire."*)
- **Filler:** "Bro." / "BRO." / "Listen to me." / "That's just MATH."
- **Honesty slip:** in Shot 2 the real, smaller number accidentally escapes, then he covers it. This is the comment-bait engine — sharp viewers catch the lie in real time.
- **Visual gag:** the background always quietly contradicts him (empty mansion, one sad machine, rented Lambo in a dumb spot).

### Three fields per shot (Cantina video editor)
1. **Opening frame** — the frozen first frame, *including the background/setting for context*
2. **Script (action)** — the motion out of that frame
3. **Dialogue** — Rocco's line, kept to ~80–90 characters (~5–8s)

**Look anchor (keep consistent every shot):** raccoon, mirrored gold aviators,
sharp navy suit, gold chains, oversized gold watch, cocky grin.

## Trending Playbook

**Post cadence:** 2–3 Rocco videos/day for the first two weeks. Volume + consistency
is how a character breaks through — the algorithm needs reps to find his audience.

**Hook discipline:** Shot 1 must land the absurd claim in the first 3 seconds. No
throat-clearing. The number *is* the hook.

**Comment bait (this drives the algorithm):**
- The **honesty slip** in Shot 2 ("okay, it's ONE machine") makes people reply to
  point out the lie. That's the whole game — arguing viewers = pushed video.
- The **"…You're welcome."** closer begs a "no thanks 💀" reply.
- Pin a comment in-character as Rocco ("link's loading, bro. relax.") to bait more.

**Consistency = recognition:** always generate from the same saved Rocco bot, always
the aviators + chains + suit, always the "…You're welcome." button. Viewers should
know it's a Rocco video before he speaks.

**Caption formula:** absurd claim + one emoji + a question.
> "he did NINE driveways in one day 🦝 is he lying"

**Hashtags:** mix broad + niche — #sidehustle #passiveincome #brainrot #ai +
the niche (#pressurewashing, #vending). Ride the hustle-tok tags, not just AI tags.

**Series framing:** brand it so episodes compound — e.g. *"Rocco's Riches"* or
*"Bad Advice with Rocco."* Run it as a Cantina Storyline so the app surfaces new
episodes to existing followers.

**Export:** pull the finished videos out of Cantina and cross-post to TikTok,
Reels, and Shorts — don't rely on the Cantina feed alone for reach.
