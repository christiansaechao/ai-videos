# ROCCO — Scripts Vol. 3: Storage Flipping & Car Flipping

70 scripts (SF-4 → SF-38, CF-4 → CF-38). Continues `rocco-content-calendar.md`
(which holds SF-1→3 and CF-1→3). Sets and format defined in `rocco-set-library.md`.

**Line format:** `N ROLE · [SET] frame detail · *action* · "Dialogue"`
**Shape:** 1 HOOK → 2 ESCALATE (honesty slip) → 3 SECRET → 4 BUTTON ("…You're welcome.")

**Guardrail note:** several car-flipping bits touch genuinely illegal territory
(curbstoning, title floating, odometer fraud, flood cars). In every one, Rocco is
the fool, the "advice" is transparently terrible, and the BUTTON lands consequences
— cop lights, a DMV letter, a court summons. Cautionary parody, never a how-to.
