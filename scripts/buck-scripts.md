# BUCK — Character Kit & Scripts (40)

The beaver who actually does the work. Buck is the **payload character** — every
real number from our research lives in his mouth. He never hypes. He states what
the job actually pays, what it actually costs, and goes back to work.

**Structural hook:** Buck's scripts are **numbered to pair with Rocco's.** `BK-PW-1`
answers `PW-1`. That makes every Buck video a ready-made stitch/duet reply, and gives
you a reason to post him — he's not a separate channel, he's the response.

## Buck's Format — "The Correction"

Deliberately the **opposite shape** to Rocco's. No escalation, no CTA, no hype.
Buck de-escalates. That contrast is the joke.

| Shot | Role | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — THE CLAIM** | Repeats the dumb claim he just heard, deadpan | Often quotes Rocco directly |
| **2 — THE COST** | What it *actually* takes — hours, gear, wear | Where the honesty lives |
| **3 — THE NUMBER** | The real figure, stated plainly | **This is the value payload** |
| **4 — THE CLOSER** | Signature: **"That's the whole job."** | No CTA. He just goes back to work. |

**Signature elements (never change):**
- Closer: **"That's the whole job."**
- He is *never* angry at Rocco — mildly amused, at most. Contempt would make him unlikable.
- He's usually **mid-task** while talking. He doesn't stop working to make a video.
- No "bro." Ever. That's Rocco's word.

**Look anchor:** beaver, hi-vis vest over faded tee, work gloves, backward cap, flat tail.

## SETS (Buck's world — competence, daylight, real equipment)

**[BUCK-GARAGE]** — A modest tidy two-car garage set up as a workshop: pressure washer, mower, ladders, coiled hoses, labeled storage bins on steel shelves, a clean workbench with tools hung on a pegboard. Warm daylight through the open door. Everything paid off, nothing flashy.

**[BUCK-DRIVE]** — A sunlit ordinary residential driveway mid-job: a work truck with a modest magnetic sign, hoses running, half the concrete visibly cleaned. Real shadows, bright honest midday light, a normal suburban house behind.

**[BUCK-TRUCK]** — Inside a clean, well-kept work truck cab: an organized clipboard of job sheets, a coffee in the holder, a labeled key ring, a neat dashboard. Morning light through the windshield.

**[BUCK-SHOP]** — A small tidy storage bay / workspace with labeled bins, a folding table for sorting, and a checklist taped to the wall. Practical fluorescent light, everything squared away.

**[BUCK-LOT]** — A commercial parking lot or strip plaza at dawn, a surface cleaner and hoses staged, the work truck parked. Cool early light, empty and quiet.

## Posting Buck

**Best use: as the reply.** Post `BK-XX-N` a day or two after `XX-N` lands, framed as
a response — "someone sent me this." The pairing is the format.

**Three ways to run him:**
1. **Stitch/duet** — Rocco's clip, then Buck's answer. Highest-performing use.
2. **Standalone** — works alone; the claim in shot 1 stands on its own.
3. **Same-video collision** — both bots in one Cantina video (Cantina supports up to
   six characters per scene). Use sparingly; it's the "event" format.

**Do not let Buck become mean.** He's amused, never bitter. The moment he's
contemptuous he stops being the guy people trust and turns into another loud voice.
