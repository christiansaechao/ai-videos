## Narration blocks

**Block 1** — It's Monday morning. You're stuck in traffic again, heading to a job that pays you the same no matter how hard you work.

**Block 2** — Now imagine a different Monday. You pull into a quiet driveway with about two hundred dollars of equipment in your trunk.

**Block 3** — Three hours later, you drive away with two hundred fifty dollars in your pocket. Cash. No boss, no office, no degree.

**Block 4** — Today we're breaking down mobile car detailing. The real costs, the real earnings, and a secret at the end that surprises everybody.

**Block 5** — Most businesses need a building, employees, and a scary bank loan. This one needs a parking spot and a bucket.

**Block 6** — The entire starter kit — shampoo, microfiber towels, wheel cleaner, brushes, and a vacuum — costs around one hundred fifty to two hundred fifty dollars.

**Block 7** — You don't even need water at first. Rinseless wash uses one gallon and a special solution. Apartment detailers use it every day.

**Block 8** — Now the fun part — the math. A basic wash and vacuum goes for fifty to eighty dollars in most cities.

**Block 9** — A full interior and exterior detail? One hundred fifty to three hundred dollars. And each job takes just two to four hours.

**Block 10** — Two full details on a Saturday is four hundred dollars. Every weekend, that's over sixteen hundred a month without touching your day job.

**Block 11** — Your cost per job? Ten to fifteen dollars in soap and product. You keep around ninety percent of every single dollar.

**Block 12** — Look out your window. Every car you see gets dirty — rain or shine, recession or boom. That demand never goes away.

**Block 13** — There are over two hundred eighty million registered vehicles in America. Most drivers have never deep-cleaned their interior. Not once.

**Block 14** — Here's the psychology: people hate cleaning their car, but they love a clean car. That gap is exactly where your paycheck lives.

**Block 15** — So why would someone pay you two hundred dollars when the car wash down the street charges twenty five? One word: convenience.

**Block 16** — Your customer never leaves their couch. They send one text, and two hours later their car looks brand new in their own driveway.

**Block 17** — Busy realtors, parents with crumb-covered back seats, business owners — they'll happily pay double to get their Saturday back. You're selling time.

**Block 18** — Your first ten customers are already in your phone. Detail your own car first, and take dramatic before and after photos.

**Block 19** — Post them everywhere — your story, the neighborhood group, Nextdoor. Then text twenty people: first five bookings get twenty percent off.

**Block 20** — Nothing stops a scroll like a disgusting seat turned spotless. That before and after photo sells for you while you sleep.

**Block 21** — Now, here's where beginners stop and pros get rich: the upsells. Pet hair removal? Forty dollars extra. Twenty more minutes.

**Block 22** — Headlight restoration takes fifteen minutes and pays fifty dollars. Engine bay cleaning, odor removal, stain extraction — thirty to seventy five each.

**Block 23** — And the king: ceramic coating. Materials cost you sixty dollars. Customers happily pay five hundred to fifteen hundred for the protection.

**Block 24** — Suddenly your one hundred fifty dollar job becomes a three hundred fifty dollar job. Same driveway. Same afternoon. Same customer.

**Block 25** — And now, the secret that changes everything. The pros don't sell washes. They sell monthly maintenance plans. Subscriptions for cars.

**Block 26** — The pitch is simple: instead of two hundred dollars twice a year, how about eighty nine a month — and your car never gets dirty again?

**Block 27** — Thirty clients on that plan is two thousand six hundred seventy dollars a month. Recurring. Predictable. Before a single new customer calls.

**Block 28** — That's when a side hustle becomes a company — a helper, a wrapped car, small fleet contracts with realtors and dealerships.

**Block 29** — So: two hundred bucks to start, ninety percent margins, customers on every street, and a clear path to real recurring income.

**Block 30** — What does a full detail cost in your city? Drop the price in the comments, subscribe, and stay on the bright side of the hustle.
