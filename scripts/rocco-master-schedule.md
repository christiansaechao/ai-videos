## The Posting Rule (this is the whole schedule)

Post in **rounds**. Round *N* = `PW-N → VM-N → SF-N → CF-N`.

That's it. Four videos per round, one per niche, so the feed never shows the same
niche twice in a row. At **2–3 videos/day** a round takes ~1.5 days, and **38 rounds
≈ 60 days ≈ 2 months.**

Pace at ~**4 rounds/week (16 videos)** and you land at 9.5 weeks. Push to 3/day flat
(21/week) and you finish in ~7 weeks with a buffer.

## Built-in Arc

The numbering isn't random — later scripts get more self-aware, so the character
deepens instead of repeating:

- **1–12** — pure hype. Establishes the archetype.
- **13–25** — reality intrudes. Logistics, costs, rejection, things breaking.
- **26–34** — the meta layer. Courses, seminars, merch, franchises — monetizing the
  audience instead of the business.
- **35–38** — cracks show. Honest year-in-review numbers, reacting to his old self,
  noticing the guy who does it properly.

**CF-36 "The Guy Who Actually Does This"** is the deliberate Buck setup — Rocco
watching a competent neighbor and being baffled by it. Post that when you're ready
to introduce the second character.

**CF-38 "Year One, Honestly"** is the season finale — Rocco takes the aviators off
and tells the truth, then puts them back on and ignores it. Good place to end a
Storyline chapter.

## Production Batching

Don't produce in posting order — **produce by niche**. All PW scripts share the same
sets, so generating PW-4→15 back to back means the same backgrounds and far less
re-prompting. Build a backlog by niche, post in rounds.

Suggested batches: 12–16 videos per session, one niche at a time.
